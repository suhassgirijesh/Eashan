Overview

At Eashan Studio, we provide professional DI (Digital Intermediate) services to deliver complete cinematic finishing for films, commercials, and premium video projects. DI is the final stage of post-production where we bring the entire project into one unified visual style by combining color correction, color grading, tone balancing, and scene matching.

It ensures every shot looks consistent in exposure, contrast, skin tones, and color temperature, even if the footage was captured in different lighting conditions or locations.

Our DI process focuses on creating a strong cinematic look while maintaining natural details and clarity. We enhance highlights and shadows, improve depth and color richness, and ensure the final output looks smooth, polished, and visually professional from beginning to end.

DI is also essential for creating a final master output that meets industry standards for OTT platforms, YouTube release, theatre screening, and commercial marketing.

With Eashan Studio's DI workflow, your project receives the final premium finishing that makes it look high-budget, visually consistent, and completely screen-ready.
