Professional Foley sound services that bring realism and cinematic depth to your film.

Overview

At Eashan Studio, we provide professional Foley sound services that bring realism and cinematic depth to your film or video. Foley is the process of creating and recording natural sound effects like footsteps, cloth movement, object handling, door sounds, and other real-life audio elements that make scenes feel real and immersive.

Many times, on-location audio doesn't capture these details clearly — that's where Foley plays a major role in creating a true cinematic experience.

Why Foley is Important?

Foley adds life to every scene. Even if the visuals are perfect, without proper Foley sounds, a film can feel flat or incomplete. With Foley, the audience feels every movement, environment, and action naturally.

It improves: Realism, Scene depth, Emotional connection, Professional cinema-quality sound

Foley Sounds We Create

    Footsteps (walking, running, sand, mud, floor, stairs)
    Cloth movements (dress, jacket, saree, costume sounds)
    Hand actions (grabbing, punching, slapping, touching objects)
    Door sounds (open/close, lock/unlock)
    Object handling (bottle, phone, bag, paper, utensils)
    Fight sequence detail sounds
    Natural movement sounds for cinematic realism
